Three Simple Steps to Your Custom Cake

Placing a custom cake order is simple, and it all happens on WhatsApp. You don’t need a finished design: a photograph, a theme or a few words is enough to begin.

  1. 1

    Share your idea or reference photo

    A picture, a colour palette, a theme, a name or a few words. Whatever shows us what you imagine.

  2. 2

    Discuss design, size, flavour and requirements

    We talk through what is possible for your occasion and suggest suitable special premium flavour options.

  3. 3

    Receive a personalised quotation

    Every cake is individually quoted, based on your design, size, decoration and requirements.

Every cake is made to order. We generally need 3–5 days’ notice, with more time for larger or more complex designs, and a 75% advance payment confirms your booking. We make cakes for celebrations in Pondicherry (Puducherry) only.
