How we work

How to Order Your Cake

Orders generally require 3–5 days' advance notice. Larger or more complex cakes may need additional time.

  1. 01

    Share your details

    Tell us your occasion, required date, preferred design and approximate cake size or servings.

  2. 02

    We confirm

    We confirm availability and design feasibility.

  3. 03

    Flavours and quotation

    We discuss suitable special premium flavour options and provide a personalised quotation through WhatsApp.

  4. 04

    Confirm with 75% advance

    Once the quotation is agreed, a 75% advance payment is required to confirm your booking.

  5. 05

    Remaining 25%

    The remaining 25% is payable according to the agreed payment terms.

  6. 06

    Delivery

    Delivery arrangements within Pondicherry are confirmed based on cake size and location.

Every cake is individually quoted, as the price depends on the cake design, size, decoration, flavour selection and your requirements.
