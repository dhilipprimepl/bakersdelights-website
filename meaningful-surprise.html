Planning a Special Surprise

Planning a customised cake for a special occasion in Pondicherry? Share who it is for, the occasion and when you need it. We’ll talk through design and flavour options and prepare a personalised quotation.

  • Booking. A 75% advance payment confirms your booking once you are happy with the quotation.
  • Individually quoted. The price depends on the design, size and your requirements.
